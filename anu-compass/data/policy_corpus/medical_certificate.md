# Medical Certificates for Assessments

## What ANU accepts
A medical certificate must be from a **registered health practitioner** (GP, psychologist, psychiatrist, dentist, etc.). It must include:

- Practitioner name, qualifications, and provider number.
- Date of consultation.
- Dates the student was **unfit for study or assessment**.
- A statement of impact (mild / moderate / severe). Diagnosis is **not** required.
- Practitioner's signature (digital signatures from clinic systems are accepted).

A receipt or appointment confirmation **alone is not a medical certificate** and will be rejected.

## Where to get one — on campus
- **ANU Medical Centre** (Health & Wellbeing Centre, Building 156, Fellows Lane). Bulk-bills students with Medicare; accepts OSHC. Walk-ins limited; book ahead. Hours typically Mon–Fri 8:30–17:00.
- **ANU Counselling** can provide a supporting letter for mental-health-related issues. Free for ANU students.

## Where to get one — off campus (when ANU services are unavailable)
- **Civic** — multiple bulk-billing GPs around Bunda St / Northbourne Ave. Same-day appointments often available.
- **Dickson** — large medical centres along Woolley St with extended hours.
- **After hours / weekends** — National Home Doctor Service, or 13SICK home-visit service.
- **Telehealth** — services such as Doctors on Demand or Hola Health can issue a certificate via video call (~15–25 minutes, fee may apply, sometimes claimable on Medicare).

## International students
- **OSHC (Overseas Student Health Cover)** is accepted at most large medical centres; confirm when booking.
- Bring your OSHC card and passport. Some clinics charge upfront and you claim back from your insurer.
- ANU Medical Centre direct-bills most major OSHC providers (Bupa, Allianz, Medibank, nib).

## Common rejection reasons
- Date of certificate does not match the assessment date.
- Certificate from a non-registered practitioner (e.g., a chiropractor only) when the issue is academic capacity.
- Receipt or pharmacy slip submitted instead of a certificate.
- Certificate written *after* the unfit period — get one *during* the illness.

## What to do today
1. Book the earliest appointment you can — ANU Medical Centre first, then Civic / Dickson if booked out.
2. Ask the practitioner specifically: "Can I get a certificate for academic Special Consideration covering [dates]?"
3. Save the certificate as a PDF and upload immediately — do not wait until the day of the deadline.
