# Medical and Health Services on Campus

## ANU Medical Centre
General practice medical service for students and staff.

- **Location.** Health & Wellbeing Centre, Building 156, Fellows Lane (Acton campus).
- **Hours.** Typically Mon–Fri 8:30–17:00. Closed weekends and public holidays.
- **Booking.** Phone or online — same-day urgent slots usually available, plan-ahead bookings open 1–2 weeks out.
- **Bulk billing.** Yes, for students with valid Medicare.
- **OSHC accepted.** Direct billing for most major providers (Bupa, Allianz, Medibank, nib). Bring your OSHC card.
- **Services.** Consultations, prescriptions, referrals, vaccinations, women's health, sexual health, mental-health care plans, medical certificates.

## Pharmacy
There is no pharmacy inside the Health & Wellbeing Centre. The closest options:

- **Acton Pharmacy** in Childers Street area, walking distance from main campus.
- **Civic chemists** (Priceline, Chemist Warehouse) — 10–15 minutes' walk from Acton.
- ANU Medical Centre can email or fax prescriptions directly to a pharmacy if mobility is an issue.

## When ANU Medical Centre is closed
- **Civic GPs.** Several bulk-billing clinics around Bunda St / Northbourne Ave with weekend hours.
- **Dickson medical centres.** Open extended hours, often 7-day.
- **Calvary Bruce Public Hospital ED.** For urgent care that is not life-threatening.
- **Canberra Hospital ED (Garran).** Major public hospital ED.
- **National Home Doctor Service / 13SICK.** After-hours home visits, bulk-billed.
- **000.** Ambulance for emergencies (chest pain, severe bleeding, unconsciousness, severe mental-health crisis).

## Sexual health and contraception
- ANU Medical Centre offers STI testing, contraception, and sexual-health advice — confidential.
- **Canberra Sexual Health Centre** at Canberra Hospital — free, walk-in, no Medicare needed. Good option for international students.

## Vaccination
- Routine vaccines (flu, COVID-19 boosters, travel) at ANU Medical Centre.
- Some are free under the National Immunisation Program; others are paid. Confirm when booking.

## Tips
- Same-day appointments fill up by mid-morning. Call right when the centre opens.
- For a medical certificate, mention it when booking so the appointment is long enough.
- International students: bring **passport, OSHC card, ANU student ID** to your first visit.
