# Course Selection, Enrolment and Census Date

## Key dates
- **Enrolment opens** several weeks before each semester. You enrol via **ISIS** (the student admin system) on `my.anu.edu.au`.
- **Census date** is the deadline to drop a course without academic or financial penalty — typically a few weeks into the semester. After census, you pay full fees and a withdrawal will appear on your transcript.
- **Last day to add** a course is usually earlier than census; check the academic calendar.
- **Last day to drop without academic penalty (WD)** is later in the semester; after that, dropping results in a Withdrawn Fail (WN).

Always check the official **Academic Calendar** on `anu.edu.au` for the exact dates of your semester.

## Choosing courses
- Use **Programs and Courses** (`programsandcourses.anu.edu.au`) to read course descriptions, prerequisites, indicative offerings, and assessment summaries.
- Use **ISIS Plan a Program** to map courses against your degree's requirement structure.
- Each course shows **prerequisites** (must complete first), **co-requisites** (concurrent), **incompatibility** (cannot take both), and offering pattern.

## Common rules
- **Standard load.** 24 units per semester (4 courses × 6 units) is full time.
- **Maximum overload.** 30 units per semester usually requires permission from your College.
- **Reduced load.** Permitted, but international students must stay in study load required by their visa unless they have approved Reduced Study Load.
- **Prerequisites enforced.** ISIS will block enrolment if you do not meet prerequisites; you can request a waiver from the convener for borderline cases.

## Degree audit
- Use **Plan a Program** in ISIS for a self-service audit of which requirements you have met.
- For a formal degree check, contact your **College Student Administration** — required if you are within one semester of expected graduation.
- Apply to graduate at least one full semester before your final exams.

## Cross-institutional study
- Want to take a course at another university (UC, USYD, Melbourne, etc.) and have it count? Apply for **cross-institutional permission** *before* enrolling at the other institution. Approval is not automatic.
- Letters of Permission take 2–4 weeks; submit early.

## Changing program
- Internal program transfers go through `my.anu.edu.au` → Forms → Program Transfer.
- Eligibility usually depends on your WAM. Check the new program's entry requirements.
- Your completed courses may **carry over** as credit; some may not fit the new program's structure.

## Things students often miss
- **Census-date trap.** A course you forgot to drop becomes a fail and a fee bill — check ISIS the day before census.
- **Prerequisite chains.** A summer / winter session course you missed may delay your degree by a full year.
- **Capstone availability.** Many capstones are offered only once per year. Plan backwards from your graduation date.
- **Honours entry.** Honours has its own application process and a WAM cut-off; do not assume an automatic continuation.

## What to do today (if you are stuck on enrolment)
1. Open Programs and Courses for each candidate course; check prerequisites and offering pattern.
2. Open ISIS Plan a Program; line your candidates up against your degree requirements.
3. If a course is full or shows a prerequisite block, email the course convener with your situation.
4. For program-level questions, contact your **College Student Administration** — they are the authority, not random forums.
