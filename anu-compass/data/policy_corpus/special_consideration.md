# Special Consideration

## When this applies
Special Consideration lets the assessor take into account circumstances beyond your control that affected your performance: serious illness, bereavement, accidents, mental-health crises, or comparable disruptions.

## Key rules
- **Deadline.** Submit within **3 working days** of the affected assessment / exam date — the sooner the better.
- **Where.** Apply through the **eForms portal** on `my.anu.edu.au` → Forms. Do not just email your course convener.
- **Evidence.** You must attach **independent third-party documentation** — typically a Medical Certificate from a registered health practitioner, or a supporting letter from a counsellor. Your own statement alone is not enough.
- **Medical Certificate requirements.** The certificate must be from a registered practitioner, state the dates you were unfit for study, and indicate severity. It does not need to disclose diagnosis.
- **Possible outcomes.** Mark adjustment, supplementary assessment, deadline shift, deferred exam, course re-enrolment, or no change. The College / convener decides; outcomes are **not guaranteed**.

## Difference from Extension
- **Extension** = extra time on a single assessment; handled by the course convener; usually self-service for short extensions.
- **Special Consideration** = broader / more serious circumstances affecting one or more assessments or exams; handled by the College.

## Common mistakes
- Applying after the assessment is already late — you may be out of time.
- Submitting "I was sick" with no medical certificate — will be refused.
- Using Special Consideration when an Extension is the right channel (or vice versa).
- Medical certificate dates that do not cover the assessment date.

## If you cannot get a medical certificate
- ANU Medical Centre is closed → use a bulk-billing GP in Civic or Dickson, or a Telehealth GP service.
- No Medicare → most large medical centres accept OSHC (Overseas Student Health Cover); confirm when booking.
- Mental-health-related → ANU Counselling can provide a supporting letter (free, but appointments may be limited).
- Cannot get any document → contact your College's Student Administration. Some circumstances accept alternative evidence (police report, hospital discharge summary, etc.).

## Next steps
1. Open `my.anu.edu.au` → eForms → Special Consideration.
2. In parallel, book the medical or counselling appointment.
3. Send a brief notice email to your course convener (does not replace the formal application).
4. Keep copies of every receipt and document.
