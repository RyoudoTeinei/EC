# Counselling and Mental Health Support

## ANU Counselling Centre
Free, confidential counselling for all enrolled ANU students.

- **Location.** Health & Wellbeing Centre, Building 156, Fellows Lane.
- **Hours.** Mon–Fri 9:00–17:00 (closed public holidays).
- **Booking.** Online via `my.anu.edu.au` → Wellbeing → Counselling, or by phone.
- **Format.** First appointment is typically a 30-minute triage; ongoing sessions are 50 minutes. Telehealth available.
- **Wait times.** Initial triage usually within 1–2 weeks; urgent slots are kept for crisis situations — say so when booking.

## What counselling can help with
- Stress, anxiety, low mood, sleep problems.
- Adjustment issues (new to ANU, homesickness, culture shock).
- Relationship difficulties, family stress, grief.
- Academic pressure, perfectionism, procrastination.
- Identity, sexuality, gender concerns.
- A safe place to talk before things escalate.

A counsellor can also write a **supporting letter** for Special Consideration if your studies have been impacted.

## After-hours and crisis support
ANU Counselling is **not a 24/7 crisis service**. If you need help outside business hours:

- **ANU Wellbeing & Support Line.** A 24/7 confidential phone line for ANU students. Available through `my.anu.edu.au` (the number is published there to keep it current).
- **Lifeline.** 13 11 14 (24/7).
- **Beyond Blue.** 1300 22 4636 (24/7).
- **13YARN.** 13 92 76 — 24/7 crisis line for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people.
- **1800RESPECT.** 1800 737 732 — sexual assault and domestic violence support, 24/7.
- **Emergency.** 000 (police, ambulance) if there is immediate risk to life.

## ANU Security
On-campus emergencies, safety walks at night, lockouts: **ANU Security operates 24/7**. Number is on the ANU website and the back of your student card.

## If you are worried about a friend
- Stay with them if they may be unsafe.
- Call ANU Wellbeing & Support Line or Lifeline together.
- For immediate danger, call 000.
- You can also contact ANU Counselling for advice on how to support someone.

## Things to know
- Counselling is **confidential**. Records are not shared with academic staff or your family without your consent (rare exceptions for safety).
- Sessions are free; there is no Medicare claim or fee.
- If you need ongoing therapy beyond ANU's scope, the counsellor can refer you to community services with a Mental Health Care Plan (via a GP).
