# Financial Support and Hardship

## Quick orientation
ANU has several pathways for students under financial pressure. The right one depends on whether the issue is **emergency**, **ongoing**, or **fee-related**.

## Emergency / short-term hardship
- **ANUSA Student Assistance** — confidential, independent. Can help with emergency food vouchers, advocacy on fees and rent, and referrals. Often the first stop.
- **ANU Student Equity Funds.** One-off small grants for unforeseen hardship (e.g., laptop broke before exam, urgent medical cost, family emergency). Apply via `my.anu.edu.au`.
- **Crisis food.** ANUSA runs a free food pantry; a brief intake is required, no need to "prove" hardship.

## Ongoing financial difficulty
- **Centrelink (Australian citizens / PR).** Youth Allowance, Austudy, ABSTUDY. Apply through Services Australia.
- **Scholarships.** Search the **ANU Scholarships portal** — many are need-based, some are merit-based, some are very specific (region, course, background). New rounds open at multiple points across the year.
- **Part-time work on campus.** Casual roles in libraries, residential halls, Student Services, research labs. Listed on **CareerHub**.
- **Tutoring, marking, demonstrating.** Available from second year onward in many courses. Email the convener of a course you did well in.

## International students
- Visa work limits: typically **48 hours per fortnight** during semester, unrestricted during official breaks. Check the latest rules — they have changed in recent years.
- International students are **not eligible for Centrelink**.
- ANU has specific hardship grants and scholarships for international students — see the Scholarships portal and the International Student Services team.
- Some embassies and home governments have hardship funds for their nationals studying abroad.

## Fee-related issues
- **Cannot pay fees by census date.** Talk to **Fees Office** (`fees@anu.edu.au`) immediately. They may offer payment plans. If you do nothing, you can be administratively withdrawn.
- **HECS-HELP / FEE-HELP** (citizens / eligible PR). Defer tuition to the tax system.
- **Refund after withdrawing.** Strict deadlines — usually only before census date. After census, you owe the full fee unless you have approved Special Circumstances.
- **Special Circumstances refund.** If something serious happened post-census, you can apply to have your enrolment cancelled and fees refunded; needs documented evidence.

## Housing
- **ResLife (residential halls).** Rent assistance, payment plans, transfer between halls if a hall becomes unaffordable.
- **Off-campus rental crisis.** ANUSA can advocate; ACT government has tenancy support; Tenants' Union ACT offers free advice.

## Tips
- Apply for **multiple** small grants rather than relying on one big one.
- Keep evidence: bank statements, bills, medical letters. Hardship grants almost always require some documentation.
- Speak up early. Most outcomes are better when you reach out before falling behind on rent or fees.

## Where to start today
1. Book ANUSA Student Assistance — they can map your situation to the right pathway.
2. Search the Scholarships portal for anything currently open.
3. If fees are due soon, email the Fees Office today, do not wait.
